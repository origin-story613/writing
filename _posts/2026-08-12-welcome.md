I post on LinkedIn. Some of that also runs on dev.to, DZone, or Hackernoon. But platforms deprioritize old content, algorithms change, and none of it is really mine — it's rented space.

This site is the canonical version. Longer, unedited by a platform's character limits, and it'll still be here in five years.

If you found this through a LinkedIn post or a repost somewhere, welcome — this is the source.

**How I use this site:**
- Full-length articles live here first
- LinkedIn gets the hook and a link back
- Reposts to dev.to / DZone / Hackernoon link back here as the canonical source
- The [Press & Reprints](/press/) page tracks where else things have run
